Sheer Khurma

by the.noramlcook

Sheer Khurma is a popular Eid dessert flavoured with ghee, variety of roasted nuts, dates, and dates infused milk. It can either be served as a semi-liquid dessert or as a thick pudding.

Ingredients

  • Full-fat milk: 500 ml
  • Ghee: 20 ml
  • Dates (chopped): 7 nos
  • Almonds (Badam): 7 nos
  • Pistachios: 7 nos
  • Cashews: 7 nos
  • Other nuts of your choice (optional)
  • Sugar: 20 gms
  • Condensed milk: 2 tbsp
  • Crushed cardamom: 2 pods
  • Thin vermicelli: 20 gms

Steps

  1. In a pan, add 500 ml full-fat milk and 2 crushed cardamom pods. Bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce the heat once milk starts boiling and add chopped dates. Let it simmer until the milk reduces to about 400 ml.
  3. Meanwhile, heat 20 ml ghee in another pan and fry the nuts (almonds, pistachios, cashews, and any other nuts of your choice) until golden brown. Remove the nuts and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, roast 20 gms of thin vermicelli in the remaining ghee until golden.
  5. Add the roasted nuts and vermicelli to the milk-date mixture. Stir well.
  6. Add 20 gms sugar and 2 tbsp condensed milk. Mix and cook for a few more minutes.
  7. For a semi-liquid consistency, switch off the flame while for a thicker consistency, cook until it all comes together as it will thicken further.
  8. Serve hot or cold.
