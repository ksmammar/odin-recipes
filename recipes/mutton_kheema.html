Mutton Kheema

by the.noramlcook
image and recipe credits: the normal cook

Mutton Kheem recipe, an excellent companion for Chapati or Ghee/Jeera/Pulao Rice.

Ingredients

  • 3 table spoons of oil/ghee/butter or mix of all three
  • 3 clove, 3 cardamom, along with a small stick cinnamon
  • 3 medium sized onions
  • 1 teaspoon of ginger garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies
  • 2 medium sized tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon of red chilli powder
  • 2 teaspoons of coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of turmeric powder
  • 400 grams of minced mutton
  • 1 diced carrot and potato
  • 1/2 teaspoon of garam masala powder
  • Required amount of salt and water

Steps

  1. Take a pan, and add 3 table spoon of Oil/ghee/butter, then add 3 clove, 3 cardamom and cinnamon stick.
  2. Add 3 medium sized onion cook for sometime, little bit salt and then add 1 teaspoon of ginger garlic paste, cook till the raw smell gone.
  3. Then add 2 sliced tomatoes and green chillies and continue cooking in medium flame.
  4. Add minced meat and red chilli powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder,garam masla powder.
  5. Finally add diced carrot and potato and required amount of water and salt.
  6. Close the pan with lid and put it in low flame.
  7. You can serve this with rice or chapati.
